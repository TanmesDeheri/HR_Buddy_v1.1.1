# Artscape Company Policy Document

---

## Notice Period Policy

* Employees are required to serve a mandatory **90-day notice period** upon resignation.
* Alternatively, employees may opt for an **equivalent salary buyout** in lieu of serving the notice period, subject to management approval.
* Early release requests are evaluated on a case-by-case basis depending on project handover status and operational continuity.
* Notice period calculations begin officially on the day the formal resignation letter is accepted by the HR department.

---

## Leave Balance Lookup Process

* Employees earn leave at the rate of **1.25 leaves per month**, accrued from January up to the current active month.
* To check your current leave balance, **please let me know how many leaves you have taken so far this year**.
* Once you share the number of leaves taken, the HR assistant will compute your balance using the formula: $(1.25 \times \text{months elapsed}) - \text{leaves taken}$.
* Approved leave requests are automatically updated in the employee portal for real-time visibility between lookup checks.

---

## Public Holiday Dates

* Artscape observes a standard calendar of public holidays applicable to all regional offices and remote team members.
* Key public holidays include New Year's Day, Republic Day, Independence Day, Gandhi Jayanti, and major regional festivals.
* A comprehensive list of the annual holiday calendar is published on the company intranet portal at the beginning of each calendar year.
* Employees working on designated public holidays are eligible for compensatory off (comp-off) as per company guidelines.

---

## Reimbursement Submission and Status Process

* Expenses must be submitted via the internal finance portal within **30 days** of incurring the cost, accompanied by valid digital tax invoices/receipts.
* Submitted claims undergo a two-step verification process involving direct supervisor approval followed by finance department audit.
* Reimbursement status updates (Pending, Approved, or Disbursed) can be tracked directly through the employee finance dashboard.
* Disbursed amounts are typically credited to the employee's registered salary account during the next scheduled payroll cycle.

---


## Internet Reimbursement Policy

* Employees working remotely or in hybrid mode are eligible for reimbursement of home broadband/internet charges, up to a maximum of **INR 1,000 per month**.
* Claims must be submitted via the internal finance portal within **30 days** of the bill date, with a valid digital copy of the internet bill showing the employee's name or registered address, billing period, and amount paid.
* Only one internet connection per employee is reimbursable; mobile data recharges, installation fees, router purchases, and late-payment penalties are not covered.
* Internet claims follow the standard two-step verification (supervisor approval, then finance audit), and status (Pending, Approved, or Disbursed) can be tracked on the employee finance dashboard.
